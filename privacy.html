Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent Monkey vs Alien development team (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the Monkey vs Alien Android game (“the Game”). Contact us at hallucinationvn@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask players to create an account or to enter a name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

The Game does not request internet access and contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting software from any third party. It does not read the device’s advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, such as completed levels, cards, coins, and settings, in a file on your device. This file never leaves the device: it is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

Google Play

If you install the Game from Google Play, Google processes information about the download and your device under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control that processing.

Children’s privacy

The Game is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, a child can play without sharing any information with us. If a parent or guardian has a question about a child’s use of the Game, contact us.

Your choices

Because we hold no information about players, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. To remove the progress stored on your device, uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings. See the Data Deletion Instructions.

Changes

If a future version of the Game collects information or adds third-party services, we will update this policy before releasing that version. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Email privacy questions to hallucinationvn@gmail.com.
