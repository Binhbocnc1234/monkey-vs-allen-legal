Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent Monkey vs Allen development team (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the Monkey vs Allen Android prototype (“the Game”). Contact us at hallucinationvn@gmail.com.

Information we handle

The Game does not currently ask players to create an account or directly submit a name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

Third-party software development kits may automatically process limited technical information, depending on device settings, region, consent, and configuration:

  • Device and app information, such as operating system, device model, app version, and language.
  • Network information, including IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Identifiers, including an advertising identifier when permitted.
  • Game, advertising, installation, attribution, and diagnostic events.
  • Consent choices and fraud-prevention signals.

Why information may be processed

  • Operate, test, secure, and improve the Game.
  • Measure installations and prototype-test performance.
  • Display and measure advertising where permitted.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, crashes, and technical problems.
  • Meet legal, platform, and consent requirements.

Third-party services

The prototype integrates services supplied or managed by the following providers. Each provider processes information under its own policy and available controls:

  • Lion Studios / LionSDK Privacy Notice
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Adjust Privacy Policy

For more information about AppLovin’s collection and use of information, visit AppLovin’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising and consent choices

Where required, a consent interface may ask whether certain advertising or analytics processing is permitted. Players may use that interface and their device privacy or advertising settings to manage available choices. Refusing optional processing should not prevent access to the core prototype.

Children’s privacy

The Game is designed to be understandable by children approximately 8–12 years old. We do not ask children to submit personal information through the Game. The current build is a prototype intended for controlled testing and should not be distributed broadly to children until child-directed advertising, SDK configuration, store declarations, and guardian-consent requirements have been verified.

If a parent or guardian believes that information about a child has been collected improperly, contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate deletion or restriction steps.

Retention

We do not currently operate a player-account database for the Game. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. We retain privacy correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to respond, demonstrate compliance, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Data rights and deletion

Depending on location, players or guardians may have rights to request access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or deletion. See our Data Deletion Instructions or contact us. We may ask for limited information needed to locate a record and verify the request, but do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary identity documents.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to this prototype. No system is completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the player’s country, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Email privacy questions or requests to hallucinationvn@gmail.com.
