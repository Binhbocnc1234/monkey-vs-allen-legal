Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Monkey vs Alien has no accounts and sends no data to us or to any third party. The only data the Game keeps is your saved progress, stored on your own device.

Delete your saved progress

  • Uninstall the Game, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → Monkey vs Alien → Storage, and tap Clear data.

Either step deletes the progress permanently. The Game excludes it from Android cloud backup, so no copy remains elsewhere.

Questions

Email hallucinationvn@gmail.com with the subject Monkey vs Alien Data Deletion. Do not send a password, payment information, or identity documents.
