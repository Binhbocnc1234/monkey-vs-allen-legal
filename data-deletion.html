Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Monkey vs Allen does not currently provide Facebook Login or maintain a player-account database containing Facebook profile information.

Request deletion

  1. Email hallucinationvn@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Monkey vs Allen Data Deletion Request.
  3. State whether the request concerns the player, a child under your guardianship, or both.
  4. Include the platform, approximate installation or play date, app version if known, and the country where the Game was used.
  5. Do not send a password, payment information, or unnecessary identity documents.

What happens next

We will acknowledge the request and determine whether we hold information that can reasonably be associated with it. We will delete or restrict information under our control when required, and provide relevant instructions for information controlled by an integrated service provider. We may retain a minimal record of the request when required for legal or compliance purposes.

Third-party provider requests

Some technical, advertising, or attribution information may be controlled by third parties. Their privacy pages describe available request mechanisms:

  • Lion Studios Privacy Notice
  • AppLovin Privacy Rights Request
  • Meta Privacy Center
  • Adjust Privacy Policy

For additional information, read the Monkey vs Allen Privacy Policy.
